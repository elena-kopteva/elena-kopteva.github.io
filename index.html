Research directions

Black holes in evolving backgrounds

Exact solutions for black holes in cosmological media, with studies of horizon structure, perturbations, and superradiance.

Inhomogeneous cosmology

Models of cosmic expansion that explore the effects of spatially varying pressure and the relation between local geometry and observation.

Agent populations and human feedback

Collective semantic dynamics in language model agents and the effects of variation in human rater state on preference data.
